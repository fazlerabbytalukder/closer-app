Delete your Closer account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Closer and go to Me → Settings → Account.
  2. Optional: tap Export memories to download your photos and answers.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email privacy@closer.app from the Google account you use with Closer, with the subject "Delete my Closer account". We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, photo, mood, status, settings) and sign-in account.
  • Photos, notes and doodles you sent, your daily-question answers and taps.
  • Your device tokens, usage counters and any exports.
  • Your pair is disconnected; remaining shared data is deleted 7 days later.

What may be kept

Purchase records required by law or by Google Play / RevenueCat for tax and accounting purposes. Cancel an active subscription in Google Play — deleting your account does not cancel it.
