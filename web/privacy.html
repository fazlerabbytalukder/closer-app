What we collect

  • Account: your Google account id, name, email and profile photo (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile: display name, photo, mood, status, time zone, language and notification settings.
  • Content you share with your person: photos, notes, doodles, captions, daily-question answers, taps, countdowns and game answers.
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can deliver messages to your widget.
  • Purchases: subscription status from Google Play via RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • Ads (free plan): Google AdMob may process device identifiers subject to your consent.

How we use it

Only to run Closer: delivering content to your paired person, showing it on widgets, sending notifications, enforcing plan limits, preventing abuse and providing support. We do not sell your data and there are no public profiles. Photo location (EXIF/GPS) data is removed on our servers.

Legal basis (GDPR)

Performance of our contract with you (running the app), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention) and consent (personalised ads, optional features).

Where data is stored

Google Firebase in the European Union (Firestore multi-region eur3, Cloud Functions in europe-west1). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Closer is not end-to-end encrypted.

Retention

Content is kept while your pair exists. After unpairing, shared content is deleted after 7 days (time to export). Deleting your account removes your profile, your content and your files.

Your rights

You can access, export (Settings → Export memories), correct and delete your data (delete your account), object to processing and complain to your data-protection authority.

Age

Closer is for adults (18+).
