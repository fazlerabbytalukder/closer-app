1. My Love Shrine
   A private, customizable digital space dedicated to your partner or best friend, featuring their photos, favorite things, shared memories, and personal messages.

2. Love Letters From the Future
   Write and schedule personal letters, messages, or photos that your partner can unlock on specific dates or special occasions.

3. I Can't Stop Thinking About You
   Send spontaneous affectionate messages to your person with one tap, expressing that you're thinking about them or missing them.

4. Always With Me Widget
   A personalized home-screen widget displaying your partner's photo, favorite memories, romantic messages, and time since you last met.

5. 100 Reasons I Love You
   Create a collection of 100 personal reasons why someone is special. Reveal one reason daily and allow both people to contribute.

6. Surprise Me
   Create unexpected digital surprises for your partner, including secret notes, surprise photos, compliments, and virtual gifts.

7. Our Love Timeline
   Create a visual timeline of your relationship or friendship, documenting important moments such as your first conversation, first date, trips, and unforgettable memories.

8. Secret Admirer Mode
   Send anonymous-style romantic messages and compliments to your paired person, with an option to reveal your identity.

9. How Much Do You Miss Me?
   Both people independently select how much they miss each other. Reveal their answers together in a fun, interactive experience.

10. Goodnight Ritual
    A nightly connection experience where both people exchange goodnight messages, voice notes, favorite moments of the day, and virtual kisses.

11. Private Chat & Voice Messages
    A private messaging space for two people to exchange text messages, photos, voice notes, emojis and replies.

12. Shared Calendar
    A shared calendar where both people can add events, plan meetups, schedule dates and receive reminders.

13. Shared To-Do Lists
    Create shared tasks, assign responsibilities, set deadlines and track completed activities together.

14. Shared Wishlist
    Create shared wishlists for gifts, restaurants, travel destinations, date ideas and things you want to experience together.

15. Interactive Photo Reactions
    React to your partner's photos, doodles and memories using animated emojis, hearts, kisses and custom reactions.

16. Virtual Pets & Shared Home
    Raise virtual pets together, collect rewards, unlock decorations and customize a shared digital home.

17. Relationship Progress & Insights
    Track relationship milestones, mood trends, shared activities, conversation participation and memorable moments over time.

18. Date Planner & Date Night Goals
    Plan virtual or in-person dates, discover activities, set weekly or monthly date goals and track quality time together.
